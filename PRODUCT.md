# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (+ minimal JS for language switch). Confirmed by user — no build step, no framework, deployed as static files behind Nginx on Coolify (Hostinger VPS).

## Users

Visitors landing on jesusjbriceno.dev: other developers, potential collaborators, recruiters, or anyone following a link from GitHub/LinkedIn. They arrive to find out who Jesús is and what he does, in seconds.

## Product Purpose

Personal landing page for Jesús Briceño. It exists to present him as a passionate developer who works across different technologies and loves learning and researching in software development and AI. Success = a visitor understands who he is, what drives him, and how to reach him, within the first viewport, and the page feels like the work of someone who cares about craft.

## Positioning

A personal statement, not a product pitch. The differentiator is the person: curiosity-driven development spanning multiple technologies, with AI as a current research interest. The page must feel built by its subject, not generated from a template.

## Operating Context

- Domain: https://jesusjbriceno.dev (currently empty; owner-managed DNS/domain).
- Deployment: Coolify on the Hostinger VPS (76.13.148.253), custom domain + compose to be configured.
- Must be multilingual from day one: Spanish (default) and English, with a clean switch, ready for an eventual international audience.
- Must stay lightweight: static files, no runtime dependencies, fast everywhere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single landing page (no multi-page site for now).
- Language switch ES/EN without page reload or with trivial reload; hreflang-ready markup.
- No tracking, no cookies, no external scripts.
- No invented claims: no job titles, employers, years of experience, projects, or testimonials may be fabricated. Copy is limited to confirmed facts and honest self-description.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jesús Briceño.
- Confirmed links: GitHub https://github.com/jesusjbriceno · LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/jesusjbriceno/ · contact@jesusjbriceno.dev
- Voice: passionate about development and AI, curious, hands-on; warm but not corporate, in both Spanish and English.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real identity and links (above). No portfolio pieces, photos, or logos provided yet. Absence: any project list, metrics, or testimonials must NOT be fabricated to fill sections.

## Product Principles

1. One page, one message: who Jesús is and why he builds.
2. Craft over decoration — the page itself is a sample of his work.
3. Bilingual by design, not bolted on.
4. Zero invented content; every claim must be his.
5. Lightweight and permanent: static, dependency-free, loads anywhere.
